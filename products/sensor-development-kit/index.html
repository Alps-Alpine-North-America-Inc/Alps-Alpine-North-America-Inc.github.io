Development Kit

What's Included

Alps Alpine Valley Development Kit Shared development platform for connecting, configuring, and evaluating the included modules. Dual Switching Magnetic Sensor Board (Qwiic) Magnetic pole-detection board built around the HGDEDM013A component. Force Sensor Board (Qwiic) Force-sensing board built around the HSFPAR304A component for load-detection evaluation. Cirque Yeti 2 (Qwiic) Capacitive-touch module with a slider, four buttons, and an X/Y touch trackpad.
Accessories Qwiic cables, power supply, and other accessories needed to connect and evaluate the bundle.
