About Us

Engineering components, sensing, connectivity, and mobility solutions for North America.

Alps Alpine North America is part of the global Alps Alpine Group and supports a broad set of markets including automotive, consumer appliances, mobile devices, and industrial equipment.

Company Direction

Alps Alpine presents its broader company mission around creating value for people and society on a brighter planet, with a vision centered on shaping a future where technology extends your senses.

Business Areas

  • Sensor and Communication
  • Mobility
  • Emerging IoT and data-driven solutions

North America Presence

The North America information page lists offices and facilities including Santa Clara, Auburn Hills, McAllen, Dublin, Redmond, and Torrance, giving the organization a broad engineering and customer-support footprint.

Who We Are

Technology development backed by a larger product and systems organization.

Across the Alps Alpine business, the public company overview emphasizes products and services spanning touch and operation, sensing and communications, and mobility-focused systems. That larger company context is what this site is meant to support: giving customers and developers a clearer path into the tools, kits, modules, and software resources connected to those technologies.

Rather than acting only as a repository landing page, this website is intended to be a development-facing front door for evaluating products, exploring technical examples, and finding the right starting points for integration work.
